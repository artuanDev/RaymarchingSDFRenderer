1. Project title and introduction

Real-Time SDF Raymarcher for Unity URP

Copy-ready introduction

I built a real-time signed-distance-field renderer to explore procedural modeling inside Unity. Instead of converting shapes into meshes, the renderer evaluates them directly on the GPU. Shapes remain editable as Unity components and can be combined through unions, subtractions, smooth blending, and spatial modifiers.

I wanted the project to work as more than a shader experiment, so I developed an authoring workflow, several shading models, PBR texture support, scene integration, debugging tools, and performance optimizations.

Component-based authoring inside Unity

Workflow caption

Every primitive, CSG operation, modifier, and material is represented by an editable Unity component. Changes are packed into GPU buffers and reflected in the render without baking a mesh. This allowed me to iterate on the sculptures directly in the scene instead of repeatedly editing shader code.

8. Explain the renderer simply

Include one small diagram:

Unity components ↓ Scene registry and change tracking ↓ GPU shape, operation and material buffers ↓ Model-bound intersection ↓ Raymarching and CSG evaluation ↓ URP lighting and final shading
Architecture summary

On the CPU, the renderer collects the active SDF components and packs their transforms, parameters, operations, modifiers, and materials into GPU buffers. The rendering pass first tests the bounds of each model, then evaluates only the relevant distance fields along the ray. Once a surface is found, its material is evaluated using the selected shading model.

This is enough architecture for most readers. Put deeper implementation details in expandable sections or your repository documentation.

9. Discuss the difficult parts

This section makes the project feel like a genuine engineering case study.

Normal mapping without mesh tangents

Traditional normal mapping relies on tangent data stored in a mesh. Raymarched surfaces do not have that data, so I had to construct a stable surface basis from the SDF normal and the shape transformation. That basis is then used to convert the sampled tangent-space normal into the lighting coordinate system.

Keeping editable scenes efficient

A component workflow is convenient, but rebuilding the complete GPU scene after every change would be wasteful. I introduced change tracking for transforms, shapes, operations, modifiers, bounds, and materials so the renderer can update only the data that has changed.

Avoiding unnecessary SDF evaluation

Evaluating every shape for every ray quickly becomes expensive. Each SDF model therefore has a world-space bound. Rays that do not intersect that bound skip the model entirely, which becomes increasingly important when several independent sculptures share a scene.

11. End with what you learned

What I took away from the project

Copy-ready conclusion

This project began as an experiment in raymarching, but the harder problems appeared around the shader itself: scene authoring, GPU synchronization, material handling, bounds, debugging, and performance. Building complete demonstration scenes helped me find problems that isolated primitive tests did not expose.

The main lesson was that a usable rendering system needs more than a correct distance function. It also needs predictable tools, clear visual feedback, and enough flexibility to support different artistic directions.

Keep the writing human

For every caption, follow this simple formula:

What is visible → why you made it → what it proves
Avoid
  • “Revolutionary rendering solution”
  • “State-of-the-art graphics”
  • “Seamlessly leverages cutting-edge technology”
  • “Highly optimized” without measurements
Prefer
  • “I built…”
  • “I used this scene to test…”
  • “The difficult part was…”
  • “This exposed a problem with…”
  • “I changed the implementation so…”

That combination of attractive results, specific technical explanations, and honest decisions will make the project read like work from a real graphics programmer rather than a shader showcase with generic marketing text.
